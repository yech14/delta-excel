Delta — Support

Version history for Excel: review every change cell by cell, save versions, revert safely.

Getting started

Open a workbook, open Delta from the Home tab, and press Take first snapshot. From then on, Review changes shows exactly what changed since your last version — added cells in green, edits in amber, removals in red — and Save version records a named point you can always come back to.

Frequently asked

Where is my history stored?
On your computer only, in Excel's local add-in storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — see the privacy policy.
I closed an unsaved workbook and my history is gone.
History is tied to the workbook file. Save the workbook at least once (Delta shows a "Save file" button while it's unsaved) so the history can reconnect to it next time.
What does Revert do?
Pressing Revert first shows a preview of exactly what would change. Nothing happens until you confirm — and the revert itself is saved as a new version, so you can even revert a revert.
Does highlighting change my formatting?
Highlight outlines changed cells and tints sheet tabs, and restores the original formatting when you un-highlight (or automatically after the 5-second flash). Formatting is not tracked in versions — only values and formulas are.
How many versions are kept?
The most recent 20 per workbook.
