Privacy Policy

Delta — version history for Excel · Last updated: August 20, 2026

Delta collects no data. Everything it saves stays on your computer. Nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.

What Delta stores, and where

Delta keeps snapshots of your workbook (cell values and formulas), your version history, and your version messages. All of it is stored locally on your device, inside the storage that Excel provides to add-ins on your machine. It never leaves your computer.

What Delta sends over the network

Nothing. Delta makes no network requests of its own — no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no accounts, no sign-in. The only network activity involved is Microsoft Excel itself loading the add-in's files, which works like opening a web page and is governed by Microsoft's privacy statement.

What Delta can access

To compare versions, Delta reads the contents of the workbook it is open in — cell values, formulas, and sheet names — and writes to it only when you ask it to (highlighting changes, editing a cell from the panel, or reverting to a version). It cannot see other workbooks, your files, or anything else on your computer.

Deleting your data

Your version history lives with Excel's local add-in storage. Removing the add-in (or clearing Office's web storage) deletes it. There is nothing to delete on any server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Delta ever adds an online feature, it will be strictly optional and off by default, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.
